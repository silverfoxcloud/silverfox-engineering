# Silver Fox Engineering

The bilingual public engineering portal of Silver Fox. It explains the architectural principles, technology choices and product boundaries behind the ecosystem.

## Content

Architecture, cloud platform, security, data, AI, operations, technology radar and public overviews of SFAS, License Platform, Fox Pay, ExoTravel and ExoHub. The English and Persian editions are independently edited. Roadmap concepts are presented as direction, not as shipped features.

## Contributing

Keep claims specific and source-grounded. Explain why a decision matters to an engineer. Review all material for public suitability; avoid private topology, credentials, internal controls, sensitive schemas and operational procedures. Keep both languages, accessibility, static export and metadata aligned with each change.
